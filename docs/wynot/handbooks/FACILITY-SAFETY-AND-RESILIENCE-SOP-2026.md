# Facility Safety and Resilience SOP (2026)

This file captures operational controls from the WYNOT facility safety SOP for
the Eagle Pass performing arts studio.

## Operational model

- Location-specific safety plan with formal induction requirement.
- Evacuation leads hold command authority and manifest accountability.
- Space consolidation rule applies when a divider splits rehearsal rooms.

## Emergency role model

- Command lead responsibilities include manifest capture, 911 contact,
  full-space sweep, and roll call at assembly.
- Crowd-control lead responsibilities include unifying divided rooms,
  assertive command delivery, and flow guidance.

## Incident protocol controls

- Severe weather protocol: move to interior shelter zones, avoid front glass,
  and avoid flood-route driving under warning conditions.
- Security protocol: lockdown for external threat, run-hide-fight escalation if
  threat breaches the site.
- Assembly protocol: primary gathering at designated cul-de-sac and secondary
  alley bypass if route is compromised.

## Training and compliance controls

- Staff and regular participants must be inducted to the localized plan.
- Signed induction logs are required.
- Management sign-off controls plan activation.

## Implementation notes for ecosystem repos

- Treat facility SOPs as first-class governance inputs for event, schedule, and
  youth-program workflows.
- Link physical safety controls with digital communication flows for emergency
  notices and attendance rosters.
