# Brand Consistency Checklist and Social Templates (2026)

This file captures enforceable branding rules for WYNOT Fitness and Gen Alpha
Academy social content.

## Visual identity controls

- Use approved logos by context and preserve ratio, clear space, and contrast.
- Use tribe-specific color palettes without cross-mixing in a single asset.
- Approved fonts only: Bebas Neue (or Anton for GA), Montserrat,
  Orbitron accents.
- Maintain dark-background-first visual direction for primary social assets.

## Voice and tone controls

- WYNOT Fitness voice: commanding, scientific, tribal, unapologetic.
- Gen Alpha Academy voice: cinematic, precise, empowering, visionary.
- Prefer identity language over activity language.
- Use short declarative sentence structure and clear CTA ending.

## Platform execution controls

- Use platform-specific dimensions and cadence plans.
- Apply accessibility fundamentals including alt text on images.
- Keep profile links, cover assets, and tribe cross-tagging current.

## Publish quality gates

- Verify resolution, contrast, logo integrity, font compliance, and tribe
  identity.
- Enforce hashtag strategy by platform.
- Require explicit CTA and final grammar proofing.
