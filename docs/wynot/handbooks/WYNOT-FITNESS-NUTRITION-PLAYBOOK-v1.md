# WYNOT Fitness Nutrition Playbook v1.0

This file captures implementation-relevant directives from the WYNOT
Macro-Mediterranean method playbook.

## Method architecture

- Layer 1: macro foundation with protein-priority targets.
- Layer 2: Mediterranean plate composition for everyday execution.
- Layer 3: MIND-aligned brain-health boosters and frequency targets.
- Optional DASH overlay for blood-pressure-sensitive members.

## Coaching deployment model

- Tier 1 beginner: awareness-first, no macro tracking at start.
- Tier 2 returner: plate templates + protein target + weekly check-ins.
- Tier 3 veteran: full macro tracking, 80/20 quality model, and diet break
  scheduling.

## Core non-negotiables

- Process adherence is the leading indicator (protein, plate consistency,
  hydration, sleep).
- No forbidden-food framing; behavior sustainability and evidence-based guidance
  are required.
- Refer-out protocol applies for medical-risk contexts.

## Safety and compliance notes

- Educational resource only, not medical advice.
- Escalate members with clinical conditions to licensed professionals.
- Preserve privacy for member nutrition and body composition records.

## Implementation notes for ecosystem repos

- Use this as a coaching-logic and content-governance reference for fitness
  tools, dashboards, and communications.
- Keep language aligned with identity-first and discipline-first voice rules.
